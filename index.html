About us

Caring for your family's smiles

Greetings! I am Dr. Adarsh Pal, owner of Shree Multispeciality Dental Clinic. My team and I are proud to offer high-quality dental care to families in Chimur, Talodhi, Wadki and the surrounding villages, using modern equipment in a comfortable and relaxed environment.

We specialise in preventive, cosmetic and restorative dentistry, from cleanings and fillings to root canals, crowns, bridges, veneers and dental implants. Every patient's needs are met with patience and care.

Our services

Complete dental care under one roof

Treatments available for patients at our Chimur, Wadki and Talodhi clinics.

Teeth Cleaning

Professional cleaning to remove plaque, tartar and stains.

Teeth Whitening

A cosmetic treatment that makes your teeth whiter and brighter.

Dental Implants

A long-lasting way to replace missing teeth and restore your smile.

Root Canal Treatment

Removes infection from inside the tooth, relieves pain and saves the tooth.

Dental Fillings

Repairs decayed or damaged teeth with tooth-coloured filling material.

Tooth Extraction

Removal of a tooth that is badly damaged by decay, injury or crowding.

Wisdom Tooth Removal

Removal of painful, impacted or crowded wisdom teeth at the back of the mouth.

Braces & Aligners

Straightens crooked teeth and corrects the bite with braces or clear aligners.

Child Dentistry

Friendly dental care and prevention for infants, children and teenagers.

Gum Disease Treatment

Treats bleeding, swollen or infected gums before they lead to tooth loss.

Cosmetic Dentistry

Improves the look of your smile with whitening, veneers and bonding.

Full Mouth Rehabilitation

Restores or replaces several damaged or missing teeth to rebuild function and appearance.

Dental Surgery

Surgical procedures such as complex extractions, implant placement and jaw surgery.

Laser Dentistry

Safe, precise laser treatment for gum reshaping, cavities and whitening.

TMJ (Jaw Joint) Treatment

Relieves jaw joint pain, clicking and stiffness to restore comfortable movement.
